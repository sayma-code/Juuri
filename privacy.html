How Juuri handles your information

We care about privacy and want this page to make it easy to understand what data is collected, why it is used, and what choices you have.

1. What information we collect

When you use Juuri, we may collect information such as:

  • Contact details you provide when you request a demo or sign up
  • Practice activity and interaction data needed to improve the experience
  • Technical information such as browser type and device details

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Juuri experience
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Understand product usage and improve our service

3. Your choices

You can contact us if you want to review, update, or delete any personal information we hold about you.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the contact form on the main site.
